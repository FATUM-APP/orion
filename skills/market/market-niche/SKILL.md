# Context

Undderstand market niche as the specific segment of the market that a service is targeting. It includes the characteristics of the ideal customer, their needs, preferences, and behaviors.

# Step by step

1. **Identify the service**: Determine which service you want to analyze and build a market niche for.

2. **Gather data**: Collect relevant data about the service, including its features, benefits, pricing, the people
that have been hired it, and the feedback received from them.

3. **Analyze the consumer**: Analyze why they hired the service, what they valued about it, and what their needs and preferences are. Look for patterns and commonalities among the consumers.


# Output

 Based on your analysis, the output should be a definition of the market niche for the service. This should include the target public, the spefcific problem that you are solving for them, if they obtained the expected results, and what is your unique value proposition that differentiates your service from competitors. You can read /references/REFERENCE.md to have an idea of what a market niche is and how to build it. Once you have created or updated the niche, save it to
`/memories/service/AGENTS.md`.
